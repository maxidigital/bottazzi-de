re.mind.

A self-tracking journal and idea catcher: speak a thought, log your mood, and keep everything in your own Google account.

remind.bottazzi.de →

re.

What it does

Ideas come at inconvenient moments. re.mind makes capturing them a single tap: record a voice note and it is transcribed and saved. You can also log your mood with a comment and location, building a personal record over time.

Your data stays yours: after signing in with Google, notes and mood entries are written to a spreadsheet in your own Google account.

How AI is used

Voice notes are transcribed with OpenAI Whisper, so you speak instead of type. Recordings made without a connection wait in an offline queue and are transcribed once you are back online.

How it is built

  • Started as a Telegram bot and rebuilt as a web app with its own interface.
  • Spring Boot backend: Google sign-in, per-user Google Sheets storage, Whisper transcription.
  • React frontend with a local cache of session and notes, so the app opens instantly without a loading screen.

Stack

React · TypeScript · Spring Boot · Java 17 · OpenAI Whisper · Google OAuth · Google Sheets API · Docker · Railway
