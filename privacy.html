What this site does

This is my personal and professional website. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Apart from the contact page described below, it loads no resources from third-party servers.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub necessarily processes your IP address and technical request data (date and time, page requested, browser and operating system) in order to deliver the page and to keep the service secure. I have no access to these logs.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is the secure and reliable delivery of this website. Data may be processed in the USA. GitHub participates in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Details are in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact form

If you write to me through the contact form, I process the name, e-mail address and message you enter in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract or steps prior to one, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is answering enquiries. I delete enquiries once they are dealt with, unless statutory retention periods apply.

The form is transmitted to a mail service I operate, hosted by Railway Corp., USA, which sends it to me by e-mail via Mailjet (Mailjet SAS, France, part of Sinch). The message is delivered to my mailbox at Google (Gmail).

To protect the form against automated abuse, the contact page loads Cloudflare Turnstile from Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Turnstile processes your IP address and technical browser data to tell humans from bots. This happens only on the contact page. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is preventing spam and abuse. Details are in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Links to other sites

Projects linked from this site, including those on subdomains of bottazzi.de, are separate services with their own data processing.
